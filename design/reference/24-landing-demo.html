What counts as VM time? Only the minutes an agent's VM is actually running. It stops by itself after 10 idle minutes and keeps its files, and a stopped VM doesn't count.







What happens when I reach a limit?
Your agents pause the work that needs more, and you get a note in your Inbox. Nothing is deleted. Upgrade, or wait for next month's allowance.


Do I need my own API keys?
No. Models, search and VMs are included in every plan. You only connect the accounts you want agents to use, like Gmail or Slack.


Can I cancel any time?
Yes. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for, then move to Free. Your agents and their history stay.
